Questions, answered

Can't find yours? Message us on Messenger.

Is it really free?

3 resume designs are free, including Save as PDF and PNG. Pro is ₱199 per month and unlocks every other resume and portfolio design.

Where is my information stored?

Only in your browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Clearing your browser data will erase it, so use Backup in the Customize panel to keep a copy.

How do I save my resume?

Tap Save as PDF or Save as PNG in the editor. The file downloads straight to your device. If a download does not start inside the Messenger or Facebook app, open the link in Chrome or Safari and try again.

What paper size does it use?

A4 by default. Open Customize, go to Paper and switch to Letter or Long bond (8.5 × 13 in).

How does Pro work?

Pay ₱199 through the GCash QR in the Go Pro window, tap the button to send your receipt to KIVO on Messenger, and KIVO sends back an unlock key. Paste the key to unlock all designs for 30 days.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The builder works on phones, tablets and computers.
